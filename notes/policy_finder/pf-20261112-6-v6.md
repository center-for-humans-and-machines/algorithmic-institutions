## Key findings

1. The finals of v4 (17.90) and v5 (17.88) tie, both about `zero` + 1.1 on the same streams, and both sweeps'
   surfaces are noise; but v5's design region as a whole averaged 15.29 against v4's 14.59, so where the region
   sits matters more than the GP peak (exploration 1).
2. Pooled over the clean round-4 points of three sweeps (197,000 games), one hit on a 3-5 giver gains +1.14
   (se 0.28) rest-of-game lead at 18 points, on a 0-2 giver +0.35 / +0.59 / +0.89 at 18 / 23 / 28, and on a 6-7
   giver -0.57; members lost at round 8 (-0.07 to -0.10 per 0-5 member hit) are back by round 12 (explorations
   2, 3).
3. Hitting 0-5 givers pays more the harder the hit, up to the largest doses tried: the predicted lead of the 0-5
   band rises from +1.11 at 14 points to +1.51 at 26, against +0.95 for v5's peak (2-5 at 12) and +0.67 for v4's;
   paired against v5's peak, 0-5 at 22 gains +0.48 (0.24) (exploration 4).
4. The band ends at 5: at amt 20 the predicted lead for hi 4 / 5 / 6 / 7 is +0.84 / +1.38 / +1.23 / +1.06, and
   6-7 givers are negative in every fit and bin (explorations 2-4).
5. Size and gap conditions do not beat a plain band: size-dependent bands score +1.41 to +1.59 vs +1.44 for 0-5
   at 22, gap-filtered ones +0.39 to +0.98 (exploration 4).
6. Newcomers (who joined at the round-3 decision, 43% of the 0-5 givers) react better to a hit (3-5: +1.26 vs
   +0.85 at 13; members at round 8 -0.03 vs -0.07 to -0.12), but at 16+ points every 0-5 class is positive, so a
   newcomer-specific rule scores no better (+1.29 to +1.30 vs +1.43) (explorations 5, 6).

## Hypothesis

Membership is the score, so a good manager punishes once and never again: at round 4, right after the first
switch decision and with 19 rounds left, it hits every member who gives little (0 or 1 up to 5 points) hard,
around 20 points. The low givers raise their giving for the rest of the game, herding pulls the group up with
them, and the members a hard hit sends away at round 7 come back by round 11. Middling and high givers are left
alone, since they cut back or leave. The hit should be hard rather than moderate, because the lowest givers move
only under 16+ points, and low-giving stayers leave as readily under a moderate hit as under a hard one. The
rule: at round 4 only, every valid member who gave between lo and hi points gets amt points.
- `lo` (int, [0, 2]): lowest contribution punished. I expect 0 or 1; the 0-givers are the noisiest class, so the
  sweep compares including them, sparing them, and sparing the 1-givers too.
- `hi` (int, fixed at 5): highest contribution punished.
- `amt` (float, uniform [14, 28]): the one-off hit in points. I expect 20-26. 14 is about v5's dose plus a
  margin, and 28 is the largest dose any sweep has tried.

### Justification

Finding 1 is why v6 stays in v5's family and moves its design region, not its GP peak: the sweep cannot resolve
differences of ~0.5, so every point of the region should be one the pooled experiment rates well. Finding 2 is
the band itself and why round 4 is the only round (the member cost is temporary). Finding 3 drives `amt`'s range
up from v5's 12.9 and keeps `lo` low: the 1-2 givers pay only at 16+, and the 0-5 band's prediction rises to 26.
Finding 4 is why `hi` is fixed at 5. Findings 5 and 6 are why there is no size, gap or newcomer condition. The
predicted lead over zero averages +1.22 over the design (min +0.94), against +0.95 for v5's peak, so I expect
v6's final to beat 17.90 by about 0.3 to 0.5, inside the noise of one final.

What would contradict it: a v6 sweep whose mean is not above v5's 15.29, or a surface falling in amt above about
18. The second would mean that hard hits cost members later in the game in a way the round-8 and round-12 sizes
did not show, or that the rising 0-2 curve was an artefact of the quadratic fit; the binned check is flatter.

Not modelled:
- A separate newcomer dose (finding 6). It matters only at doses below the range.
- Own size (finding 5). In small groups the 0-2 givers are negative below 18 points, but the range starts near
  there.
- Any second hit at rounds 8 or 12. v5 found these negative or unreliable, and no sweep since has varied them.
