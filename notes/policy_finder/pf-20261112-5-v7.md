## Hypothesis

On this stack the first switch decision sets the group sizes for the rest of the game, and the
switch model reads a manager who punishes several of a group's members before that decision as
a reason for the group to leave, while a single member's shock is read as that member's matter
and buys its full contribution answer. A good manager therefore shocks at most one or two
members before the first decision - the lowest contributors - hard, and then, once membership is
set, shocks every low contributor right after the first decision (round 4), when no count
penalty applies and the trained habit has 19 rounds to pay; round 8 is optional and nothing is
dealt in any other round. The rule: in round 0 the K0 lowest contributors among my members who
timed in, are not alone and gave at most `hi` get `D0`; in rounds 4 and 8 every such member
giving at most `hi` gets `D4` (`D8`).

- `D0` (float, [10, 30] uniform): the round-0 shock to each of the lowest contributors. Expect 20-30.
- `K0` (int, 1..3): the most members shocked at round 0. Expect 1-2.
- `D4` (float, [15, 30] uniform): the round-4 shock. Expect 25-30.
- `D8` (float, [0, 30] uniform): the round-8 shock. Expect 0-15, small effect.
- `hi` (int, 4..7): the highest contribution shocked. Expect 5-6.

### Justification

Finding 1 is why I looked for a structural lever rather than tune doses again, and finding 3 is
that lever: v3-v6 hit every low contributor at round 0, a mix of the 1-member case (+2 per round)
and the 3+-member case (-3..-9), which explains D0's flat surfaces; `K0` separates them. Finding
2 explains why a count effect before the first decision matters so much (members moved there
stay moved) and finding 4 why the cap is applied at round 0 only. K0 3 is in practice the v3-v6
rule (4+ low contributors in 1.5% of games), so the sweep can reject the idea; I expect 1-2
(the 2-member cells straddle zero). D0 starts at 10 because one-member shocks pay more at 20-30
than at 10-20 (finding 3). D4's range [15, 30] follows finding 4 and v5/v6's surfaces (best
26-30). D8 keeps 0 reachable (finding 5). hi spans 4..7 around v3-v5's 5-6 optimum; with the cap
a higher hi only widens whom round 0 can pick. What would contradict it: a sweep peak at K0 3, or
`members` not rising as K0 falls (the pair cells come from games whose later policies may still
differ within 10 points, and the 2-member cells are noisy). Not modelled: a size gate on the
round-4 shock (it pays only in groups of 6+ in exploration 2, but those are 2/3 of the cases and
the rest are near 0), a count cap at rounds 4 and 8 (finding 4: none needed), shocks in rounds
1-3 and from round 9 on (negative in v3-v5), and the other group's state. Expected gain over v3:
+0.3..+0.7 pool points, near the paired final's resolution.
