1. ## Hypothesis

A good manager on these clones shocks the group early with a convex dose on everyone below full contribution (30 for a free rider, ~6 for a half contributor, 2-3 for a contributor at 16, a point near 19) for about six rounds, then keeps the full dose only for relapses to free riding until ~10 rounds before the end, then stops. The early shock buys the rise from ~11.5 to ~17; the first points on near-full contributors are the cheapest contribution per point; the free-rider policing stops the herding decay at the cost of rare big doses; and after ~round 14 no dose can repay its double cost. Six versions show the family is the right shape and exhausted at ~656; v7 places it where the pooled, slot-corrected evidence of all three earlier sweeps puts the optimum and concentrates the sweep there. The rule: `pmax (1 - c/20)^g`, g = log2(pmax/d10), for c <= 10, `d10 ((20 - c)/10)^k`, k = log(d10/d16)/log(2.5), for 10 < c < 20, nothing at 20, clamped at 30; all valid players in rounds t < horizon, players with c < lthr in rounds horizon <= t < lstop, nobody after.
- `pmax` (float, fixed 32): the zero contributor's dose before the clamp; flat on every evidence.
- `d10` (float, [4.5, 7.5]): the dose for a half contributor; I expect 5.3-6.3.
- `d16` (float, [1, 4]): the dose for a contributor at 16, the tail; I expect 2-3.2.
- `horizon` (int, [5, 8]): the shock's length in rounds; I expect 6 (perhaps 7).
- `lthr` (int, [2, 6]): free-rider line after the shock (c < lthr); I expect 3-5.
- `lstop` (int, fixed 14): first round without punishment; flat over 13-15.

### Justification

Finding 2 says the structure is right and the remaining differences are in placement, and finding 3 removes the last structural idea I had (concentrating punishment for its spillover), joining the earlier versions' negative tests of history-dependent levers; so I kept v6's code. Finding 4 sets the ranges: each swept parameter's pooled optimum sits inside its range with at least one level or ~0.5 of a dose unit on each side (d10 peak 5.5-6 in [4.5, 7.5]; d16 2-3.2 in [1, 4]; horizon 6 in [5, 8]; lthr 4 in [2, 6]), and the cut-off ends are the regions that cost 1-1.5 (d10 > 7.5, d16 < 1, horizon 4 or 9). Finding 1 is why I fixed pmax and lstop rather than sweep them: with a seed-slot noise of sd 2.6 per point, density matters more than coverage, and those two axes are flat (v6's landscape gave lstop an effect of 0.00, the pooled surface 0.05 over 13-15). The hypothesis would be contradicted if the sweep's peak ran to an edge: d10 at 4.5 or d16 at 4 would mean the tail-heavy direction goes further than the pooled data saw; d10 at 7.5 with d16 at 1 would bring back v5's thin-tail end. Not modelled: history-dependent doses, group-relative standards, tapers, re-shocks, late nudges and post-lstop doses (tested in v3-v5 without gain), concentration of punishment (finding 3), and timeouts (cannot be punished, no lever found). I expect ~656-656.5 on the clones: level with the bar, not a step beyond it; a clear gain would need a mechanism the clones' inputs (previous contributions, punishments and timeouts, no round number) do not seem to offer.
