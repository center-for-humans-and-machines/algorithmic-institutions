## Explorations

1. **Where does v4 stand?** Read v4's notes, rule and sweep (`final/sweep.json`, `landscapes.json`). v4's late phase paid on the clones: its fitted peak (thr 24, pmax 30, gamma 2.9, horizon 7, lthr 4, lstop 15) scored 655.9 (se 1.2) on fresh seeds and s187 (thr 23, pmax 29.4, gamma 2.6, horizon 8, lthr 3, lstop 13) 656.6, against v2's 651.9; so the bar is now ~656. Its surrogate forecast (~654-655 on the clones) was right to within ~1. The landscape puts thr at the top edge (24) and gives thr and gamma by far the largest effects (17 and 19 points) with short length scales; they trade off along a ridge (high thr with high gamma and low thr with low gamma give the same doses for c 0-15: the top sweep points include thr 18 / gamma 1.8 at 658.3 and thr 23 / gamma 2.6 at 657.8). With c = 20 never punished, thr above 20 only reshapes the curve, so the edge at 24 is a symptom of the parametrisation, not of a standard above 20. The surrogate was saved in /tmp and is gone; I am refitting it (scripts/policy_finder/pf-20261105-3/v5_surrogate.py) on all four sweeps, now including v4's 256 late-phase rules, which is exactly the data the v4 surrogate lacked. Next: look at v4's best games round by round to see where payoff is still lost.

2. **Where does v4's best rule still lose payoff?** scripts/policy_finder/pf-20261105-3/v5_rounds.py on v4's final per_round.parquet and the reference. Next, once the surrogate is refit: check it on v4's sweep, then test (i) a parametrisation of the dose without the thr/gamma ridge, (ii) levers aimed at (a) and (b).

4. **First screen of new levers on the surrogate.** scripts/policy_finder/pf-20261105-3/v5_levers.py group `a`, 3 seeds x 3000 games with common random numbers. v4's peak 653.8 and s187 653.9 (equal, as on the clones within 0.7). ## Key findings

1. v4's late phase paid on the clones: its peak scored 655.9 and s187 656.6 on fresh seeds against v2's 651.9; v4's landscape put thr at the top edge (24), with thr and gamma the two largest effects (17 and 19 points) along a ridge (exploration 1).
2. A surrogate refit on all four sweeps (1024 rules) reproduces v4's sweep with rmse 3.5 and its six finals within 2.3, though not in order: it resolves ~2 points (exploration 3).
3. No new lever clears that resolution: early nudges on c 16-19 (+0.2 to +0.6, -1.4 when larger), re-shocking slipping groups (-0.7 to -2.8), dosing mid-level droppers late (-1.4), free-rider doses after lstop (-1.1), relapse-only late doses (-0.3), a tapered or group-dependent shock (-1.2 to -5.2), a group-relative shock standard (-0.9 to -4.7) (explorations 4, 5, 7).
4. The shock's dose curve matters only through its middle: at fixed dose for c = 10 (d10) the tail near 20 changes the score by at most 0.3, while d10 4.5 / 5.5 / 8 / 9.5 / 11 cost 5.4 / 1.8 / 0.7 / 2.9 / 6.9 against d10 7; pmax 25-36 is within 0.7 (explorations 6, 8, 9).
5. Re-read in d10 terms, v4's clone landscape also peaks at d10 ~7 (thr 22 / 23 / 24 at gamma 2.93 give d10 5.1 / 5.6 / 7.0 and 653.5 / 655.5 / 657.2), so its thr edge was the d10 contour, not a call for a different shape (exploration 8).
6. The timing parameters sit on a broad interior plateau on both the clones and the surrogate: horizon 5-9, lthr 2-6, lstop 11-18 all within ~1 of the peak; lthr 0 (no late phase) costs ~4 (explorations 6, 7).

## Hypothesis

A good manager on these clones does what v4 does: a short, flat shock in the first ~7 rounds, dosing every player below 20 on a convex curve (about 30 for a free rider, 7 for a half contributor, 1-2 near 16 and nothing at 18-20), then a late phase that keeps the big dose only for relapsing free riders until ~9 rounds before the end, then nothing, because a late dose needs ~5 rounds to repay. Six other refinements did not beat it, so v5 changes the coordinates rather than the shape: the curve is pinned by its two ends that matter, the dose for a free rider and the dose for a half contributor, so the sweep's most sensitive direction (v4's thr/gamma ridge) becomes one axis and thr's spurious edge goes away. The rule: `dose = min(30, pmax (1 - c/20)^gamma)`, `gamma = log2(pmax / d10)`, no dose at c = 20; every valid player gets it in rounds t < horizon, players with c < lthr in rounds horizon <= t < lstop, nobody after.
- `pmax` (float, [22, 40]): dose for a zero contributor before the clamp at 30; I expect 25-34 (surrogate flat 25-36, v4 clones 26-34).
- `d10` (float, [4, 11]): dose for a half contributor, setting the curvature; I expect 6-8 (surrogate peak ~7, clones ~7).
- `horizon` (int, [4, 10]): first round of the late phase; I expect 6-8.
- `lthr` (int, [1, 7]): the late phase's free-rider line (c < lthr); I expect 2-5.
- `lstop` (int, [10, 20]): first round without punishment; I expect 13-16.

### Justification

Findings 1 and 6 keep v4's structure and set the timing ranges around a plateau that both the clones and the surrogate show (horizon 5-9, lthr 2-6, lstop 11-18), with a margin of 1-2 on each side; lthr starts at 1 because finding 6 and v4's clone sweep both show that switching the late phase off (lthr 0) costs ~4. Finding 3, measured with the instrument of finding 2, is why no new lever is added: none clears the surrogate's ~2-point resolution, and the one that came closest (a near-full nudge) vanished once the curvature was right. Findings 4 and 5 justify replacing thr and gamma by d10: the score depends on the curve's middle, not its tail, and v4's edge was the d10 contour; d10's range 4-11 covers the -2 to -7 points either side of the peak, and pmax 22-40 the flat band with its -1.5 edges. It would be contradicted if the sweep put d10 at an end of its range or the best points at horizon 10 with lstop 10 (no late phase), or if the fresh-seed final fell clearly below v4's 655.9-656.6, which would mean the tail near 20 (fixed at 0-2 points here, at 1-3 in v4's peak) mattered on the clones although it did not on the surrogate. Not modelled: a separate late dose (merged in v4 at a cost of 0.15), the tail of the curve (finding 4), nudges, group-dependent or tapered shocks, and every history-dependent refinement of findings 3 and v4's notes; timeouts (a timed-out player cannot be punished). With five parameters instead of six, the 256 points sample the d10 axis more densely, which is where the gain of this version should come from: I expect ~656-657 on the clones, level with or a point above v4, not a step change.
