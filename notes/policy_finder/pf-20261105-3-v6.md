## Key findings

1. v4 and v5, the same structure in two parametrisations, are within noise of each other on fresh seeds (v4 655.9 / 656.6, v5 653.9 / 655.9, se ~1.25), and their sweeps' top points regress by up to 7 points on fresh seeds: the family is at ~655 +- 1 and selection noise is as large as the remaining differences (exploration 1).
2. The clones see only the previous contributions, punishments and timeouts of the group through a small RNN, with no round number: the late decline is dynamics, not end-game anticipation (exploration 1).
3. Pooling v4's and v5's 512 clone points, the plateau sits at d0 30, d10 6.3-6.9, horizon 6-7, lthr 4, lstop 14-15, and at that middle and timing a tail with c 16 dosed 1.5-2 fits ~1.5 better than one with c 16 at 0-0.5 (655.0 vs 653.4); but in both earlier forms a fat tail came only with a heavy middle (no point with d16 >= 2.5 has d10 below 8.8), so the tail is not identified (exploration 2).
4. In the clone sweeps' shock rounds, near-full contributors given a first point rise against an unpunished slide (c 16-17, others 14-17: three rounds later -0.52 at p 0, +0.24 at 1, +0.52 at 2, +0.64 at 3; c 14-15: -0.13 / +0.66 / +1.22 / +1.58), with diminishing returns, enough to repay one point if it holds for ~5 rounds; this is confounded with the harder doses such rules give the others (exploration 3).

## Hypothesis

A good manager on these clones shocks the group early with a convex dose on everyone below 20 (30 for a free rider, ~7 for a half contributor), then polices only relapses to free riding with the same big dose until ~9 rounds before the end, then stops, because a point costs the group 2 and a contribution point returns only 0.6 per later round. What v1-v5 left unresolved is how much the players just below full contribution need: their slide is the largest remaining loss, and a point or two may stop it cheaply, but every earlier form tied that tail to the middle of the curve. v6 frees it: the dose is `pmax (1 - c/20)^g` with g = log2(pmax/d10) for c <= 10 and `d10 ((20 - c)/10)^k` with k = log(d10/d16)/log(2.5) for 10 < c < 20, nothing at 20, clamped at 30; every valid player gets it in rounds t < horizon, players with c < lthr in rounds horizon <= t < lstop, nobody after.
- `pmax` (float, fixed at 32): the dose for a zero contributor before the clamp; flat across 24-40 on every evidence, so not swept.
- `d10` (float, [4.5, 9]): dose for a half contributor, the curve's middle; I expect 6-7.5.
- `d16` (float, [0.3, 3.5]): dose for a contributor at 16, the tail; I expect 1-2.5 (c 16 nudged by 1-2 points, c 18-19 by 0-1).
- `horizon` (int, [4, 9]): first round of the late phase; I expect 6-7.
- `lthr` (int, [2, 6]): the late phase's free-rider line; I expect 3-5.
- `lstop` (int, [12, 18]): first round without punishment; I expect 14-16.

### Justification

Finding 1 is why the structure is kept and the ranges are narrowed: v1-v5 explored the shape of the shock, its timing and a dozen history-dependent levers, and none beat this family; with selection noise as big as the effects, density on the plateau is worth more than coverage of regions known to lose. Finding 3 sets the d10, horizon, lthr and lstop ranges around the pooled optimum with a margin of one to two units, and is the reason for d16: the one direction the clone data favour but cannot separate from the middle. Finding 4 gives the mechanism and the range of d16 (gains taper after 2-3 points, and earlier versions' 2-4 point standing nudges and warns lost, so 3.5 is an ample top). Finding 2 says there is no end-game anticipation to exploit, so the timing stays a fixed schedule. pmax is fixed because its effect is below the sweep's noise. The rule nests v5 (d16 = d10 0.4^g, ~0.9 at d10 7), so the sweep can fall back to it. It would be contradicted if the sweep put d16 at its bottom (the tail is worth nothing, as v5's surrogate said) or its top (the near-full players want a standing nudge after all), or d10 at an edge. Not modelled: history-dependent doses, group-relative standards, tapered or group-dependent shocks and nudges after the shock, all tested in v3-v5 without gain; timeouts, which cannot be punished and showed no lever. I expect ~656-657 on the clones: level with v4 and v5, perhaps a point above if the tail matters, not a step change.
