## Hypothesis

A good manager fines clear relative shirkers firmly and often, early in the game, as long as its group is not behind in the per-capita race and is not small. A fine of 4-6 points on someone 3-5 points below the rest of the group raises what he and the group give by more than it costs, and while my group is ahead per capita and has 3-4+ members, it does not push members out (it may even make the group more attractive, since its per-capita pool rises). Small fines (1-3) are too weak to change behaviour and just cost; a high shortfall line fires too rarely to matter. v7 keeps v6's rule and moves its design into the corner where v6's sweep earned and past its open edges.

The rule (`configs/managers/rule_based/pf-20261112-2-v7.yml`): a valid member of my group is fined `fine` if the other valid members' mean contribution this round exceeds his by at least `gap`, my block-to-date per-capita pool leads ah's by at least `lead`, my group has at least `n_min` members, and t <= 4*last_block - 1.
- `lead` (float, [-2, 10]): race line in per-capita pool points. Expect 0-6.
- `gap` (float, [2, 6]): shortfall line in contribution points below the other members' mean. Expect 3-5.
- `fine` (float, [3.5, 8], floored): flat fine. Expect 4-6.
- `last_block` (int, [3, 6]): no fines after round 4*last_block - 1. Expect any; most spend is early.
- `n_min` (int, [3, 4]): smallest group size at which I fine.

### Justification

Finding 1 says the sweep's GP cannot resolve this rule's landscape, so the expected final is roughly the design's average paired gain; finding 2 shows that average was +0.25 in v6 while finding 3 shows a corner at +1.0 to +1.4. Concentrating the design there (about 23% of its volume, against 4% in v6, the rest extending the corner past its open edges) is the most direct way to raise the final. `gap` [2, 6] and `fine` [3.5, 8] follow finding 3: both gains peaked at v6's range edges (gap 3, fine 6), so the ranges keep the known-good part and extend past it, stopping at 2 and 8 because earlier versions (v4) found fines on members close to the mean and very large fines costly. `n_min` [3, 4] follows finding 4 (2 and 5 lose half the gain). `lead` [-2, 10] follows finding 4 (0-12 good, best near 0, a small step below 0 untested). `last_block` [3, 6] is kept wide because finding 4 shows no trend. It would be contradicted if v7's sweep, paired the same way, showed gap 2-3 or fines 6-7 well below the 3-5 / 4-5 core (then the corner is a peak, not an edge), or if the corner's gain shrank on the new seeds (then entry 3 was selection on noise across 15 points). Not modelled: a fine growing with the shortfall (the flat fine's step between 3 and 4-5 then plateau gives no sign of a dose response worth a parameter), and any change to the rule's structure, so that v6's corner is reproduced exactly inside v7's design. Caveat: the corner estimate rests on 15-29 points with SE 0.14-0.22 and on the per-game baseline covering ~92% of fired games.
