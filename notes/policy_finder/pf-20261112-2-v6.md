## Hypothesis

A fine on a clear relative shirker buys back part of his contribution, and its cost is the members it pushes toward ah's group. That cost is small when my group is comfortably ahead in per-capita pool (members follow the per-capita gap and are far from the door) and when the group is not small, and large in a close race, when behind, or in a group of 2-3 that one exit can empty. The fine also needs time to pay back, so the last blocks are a loss. So a good manager fines moderately (a few points) and fairly often: anyone clearly below the rest of the group, whenever the group leads the race by a moderate margin and is of normal size, through most of the game. v6 keeps v5's rule and adds a group-size floor, with ranges moved to where v5's sweep earned.

The rule (`configs/managers/rule_based/pf-20261112-2-v6.yml`): a valid member of my group is fined `fine` if the other valid members' mean contribution this round exceeds his by at least `gap`, my block-to-date per-capita pool leads ah's by at least `lead`, my group has at least `n_min` members, and t <= 4*last_block - 1.
- `lead` (float, [0, 15]): race line in per-capita pool points. Expect 5-10.
- `gap` (float, [3, 12]): shortfall line in contribution points below the other members' mean. Expect 4-7.
- `fine` (float, [1, 6]): flat fine. Expect 2-4.
- `last_block` (int, [3, 6]): no fines after round 4*last_block - 1. Expect 4-6.
- `n_min` (int, [2, 5]): smallest group size at which I fine (2 = no floor). Expect 3-4.

### Justification

Finding 1 sets the bar and anchors the design: v5's final point is inside v6's space (n_min 2 reproduces v5), and its two edge values (gap near 5, last_block 6) are why gap now reaches down to 3 and last_block keeps 6. Finding 2 and 4 set `lead`: the point-level gain is at lead lines 0-8 and the per-game gain turns positive from a race of ~7.5, so [0, 15] centres the likely best (5-10) and drops v5's 15-25, which was nearly never-punish. Finding 2 sets `gap`: the gain grew as the line fell toward v5's floor of 5, so [3, 12] lets the sweep test lines below 5 that no version tried, while 12+ (rarely fires, ~0) is cut. Finding 2 and 4 set `fine`: 3-6 at the point level, 2-4 at the first fine, 2.3 in the final; [1, 6] drops the never-punish sliver (the bar is no longer never-punish). Finding 4 says late first fines lose but finding 1 and 2 favour long windows, so `last_block` [3, 6] lets the sweep choose. Findings 3 and 4 give `n_min`: small groups lose in every race state, so a floor of 3-4 should help, with 2 (off) as the contrast. It would be contradicted by a peak at gap 3 (the shortfall line should go lower still or the rule should fine everyone below the mean), lead near 0 (the race does nothing beyond "not behind"), or n_min 2 (size adds nothing once the race is in). Not modelled: a fine growing with the shortfall or the lead (finding 4 shows no clear dose response, and a sixth parameter thins 256 points further), ah's empty group as a special case (v5 finding 5: it is the extreme of the race), and a separate endgame rule (last_block covers it). Caveat: the cells come from rules that also fined later in the same games, and v5's sweep landscape was too noisy for its GP to resolve, so the per-point gains of +0.3 are about the size I expect, with a final of +0.3 to +0.6 over never-punish at best.
