# Seed ensemble vs the contribution copula: is epistemic uncertainty the shared error?

Branch `auto/copula-seed-ensemble`, based on `origin/auto/punisher-current-contribution` (the fixed punisher, the RCE row, the PR #181 stimulus-skip contributor trunk and the copula scripts). A measurement, not an experiment: no model is proposed for the ledger, and no §2 gate applies. One of three parallel experiments on the "copula question" (siblings: `auto/copula-closed-loop-variance`, `auto/copula-missing-state`).

## 1. The question

The contribution copula (`src/aimanager/generic/copula.py`, `GraphNetwork._predict_encoded_copula`) injects one shared standard-normal latent per (episode, group), at weight `sqrt(rho)`, into every member's inverse-CDF draw, and carries it across rounds with `phi = 1` (a static episode latent). On the PR #181 stimulus-skip trunk the recalibrated dose is `rho = 0.0395` (95% CI [0.0181, 0.0566], `phi_hat` 0.81, shipped `phi = 1.0`; `..._stimulus_skip_herding_copula/calibration/copula_params.json`). Its justification, from the maintainer's comment on PR #140, is that members of a group co-move for two reasons — they respond to the same situation, and they share the model's error — and that only the second is a sampling problem; PR #140's Option 1 proposed to size that shared error by an *ensemble* ("draw one model at random per run ... its size comes from how much the ensemble members actually disagree rather than from a hand-tuned constant"). Nobody has measured how much the members would disagree. This branch does: is parameter (epistemic) uncertainty — the part of the model's error that a different training seed changes — negligible relative to the copula dose, comparable, or larger?

## 2. Setup

**Ensemble.** `configs/training/artificial_humans/contribution/group_switching_contribution_50ep_vnode_stimulus_skip_seed_ensemble.yml`: the shipped PR #181 config byte-for-byte (node+edge+rnn, hidden 20, group vnode, stimulus skip, `prev_contribution` / `prev_punishment` numeric, `agent_group` one-hot, lr 3e-4, weight decay 1e-5, 575 epochs, batch 4, grad clamp 1, 5-fold CV then a full-data fit, flip-doubled `experiments/2group_8agent_50ep.csv`, mask `contribution_valid`), with a config grid over `seed: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]` (the shipped artifact used 38381). The seed drives the parameter initialisation, the batch order, the training mask-pattern draws and the CV partition; the saved artifact is the full-data fit in every case, so every member sees the same 100 doubled episodes. Raven GPU, isolated dir `~/repros/ai-runs/copula-ensemble` (`AI_REMOTE_DIR`), five parallel SLURM jobs 30308939 / 30308943 / 30308946 / 30308948 / 30308949 (`train_cluster.sh ah`, `WANDB_MODE=disabled` added to the isolated dir's `.env`), one A100 each, about 6.5 epochs/s, i.e. about 90 s per fold and 10m15s–10m21s per job (six trainings each; inside PR #181's 8m43s–11m12s range). Artifacts committed under `artifacts/artificial_humans/group_switching_contribution_50ep_vnode_stimulus_skip_seed_ensemble/model/*__seed_k.pt` (LFS, ~36 kB each) with their metrics parquets.

**Measurement.** `scripts/data_analysis/copula_seed_ensemble.py` (CPU SLURM wrapper `copula_seed_ensemble.slurm`, Raven, PyG needed for the forward pass). Data through the copula script's own loaders (`contribution_copula_rho.load_full` / `select_split`): `create_torch_data` on the full file, restricted to the 40 single-copy episodes of the baseline train split (`train40`, the population the copula's rho was fitted on) and to the 50-episode canonical single-copy frame (`canon50`), rows where `contribution_valid`. Every member and the shipped trunk are teacher-forced (`predict_independent(sample=False, reset_rnn=True)`) on the same rows, giving one 21-level marginal `P_k(i)` per member `k` and agent-round `i`; `E_k(i)` is its expected value, `P_bar` and `E_bar` the ensemble means. A cell is one (episode, round, agent_group), exactly the copula's cell.

- (a) *Disagreement per agent-round:* `sd_E(i)` = SD over the five `E_k(i)` (ddof 1); `tv(i)` = mean over the ten member pairs of the total-variation distance `0.5 * |P_k(i) - P_l(i)|_1`. Reference: `sd_pred(i)` = SD of the ensemble-mean marginal `P_bar(i)` (one model's predictive spread), and `|y_i - E_bar(i)|`, the human residual.
- (b) *Shared part:* deviations `d_ik = E_k(i) - E_bar(i)`; per member the pair-weighted within-cell correlation of `d_ik` across group members (`punishment_copula_rho.rho_pairs`, the moment estimator the copula scripts print as their diagnostic), averaged over members, with an episode-cluster bootstrap CI (200 resamples). The same on the copula's latent scale: `z_ik = Phi^-1(F_bar_i(E_k(i)))`, the member's expected value mapped through the ensemble-mean model's CDF (made continuous by linear interpolation inside each level's cell), then the within-cell correlation of `z_ik - mean_k z_ik`, and `v` = between-member variance of `z_ik` averaged over rows. Two translations to a copula rho: (i) *inferred*, the additive-shift model `rho_impl = corr_z * v / (1 + v)` (a shared latent shift of variance `v` on top of the unit idiosyncratic latent); (ii) *measured*, the copula calibration's own pairwise-likelihood MLE (`punishment_copula_rho.mle_on_rows`, the estimator that produced 0.0395) run on synthetic data generated by the ensemble-as-noise model — one member drawn per episode, every agent-round drawn independently from that member's marginal — with `P_bar` as the marginal model; 20 replicates, plus a null of independent draws from `P_bar`. Alongside, the copula rho refit on the *human* data with `P_bar` as the marginal (cluster bootstrap CI), and PR #140's residual within-group correlation recomputed as the within-cell correlation of `y_i - E_bar(i)`.
- (c) *Persistence:* the cell-mean deviation `D_k(e, r, g)` = mean over the cell's members of `d_ik`; its correlation with the same (episode, group) cell at round `r + lag` (lags 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, pooled over members); its ICC(1) over rounds within (episode, group); and the copula script's cross-player lag-1 pairing on `d_ik` (`contribution_copula_rho.cross_pairs`, different participants only), whose ratio to the within-round correlation is the ensemble's `phi` analogue.

**Bonus closed-loop run.** `src/aimanager/simulation/ensemble_ah.py::SeedEnsembleAH` — a contribution sampler that holds the five bare members and draws one at random (Python's global RNG, seeded by the sim seed) whenever `reset_rnn` is true, i.e. once per episode, then samples every agent-round independently from it (rho 0 on every member); `load_ah_model` dispatches `*.ensemble.yml` to it. Sim config `configs/simulation/manager_testing/23_2g8a_contr_stimulus_skip_seed_ensemble_self_gnncopar1_contr_gnn_switch_curpun.yml`: the re-baseline's case-b config with only `contribution_model` (the member list), `output_dir` and `figure_name` changed; protocol, joint-exodus switch predictor and current-contribution severity-copula punisher byte-identical. Evaluated locally with the 22-row suite against case b's post-fix baseline (mean 1.0357, CG 1.55, RCE 0.8942, 13/22 rows <= 1).

## 4. Verdict

**Epistemic uncertainty, as a five-seed ensemble measures it, is small relative to the copula dose — about one sixth of it, and below the copula's confidence interval — and it has the wrong time structure.** Measured: the members disagree by 0.38 contribution points per agent-round (2% of one model's predictive variance, 2% of the human residual variance); the disagreement is genuinely shared within a group (within-cell correlation 0.16 on the expected-value scale, 0.20 on the latent scale, CIs excluding 0), but its size on the copula's scale is a rho of 0.005–0.009 against the fitted 0.0395 [0.018, 0.057]; the copula rho refit on the human data with the ensemble mean as marginal is unchanged (0.040); the cell-level deviation decays with a half-life of about a round (lag-1 0.53, lag-5 0.15, ICC 0.14) where the shipped copula assumes a static episode latent; and in the closed loop, one member per episode behaves like no copula at all (CG 2.68 vs the copula's 1.55 and PR #179's copula-free 2.40; RCD 2.40 vs 1.31; mean 1.224 vs 1.036).

**What that implies for a Bayesian / ensemble replacement (inferred).** Drawing one posterior sample per episode cannot supply the shared error the copula supplies unless the posterior is six to eight times wider on the latent scale than this ensemble (`v` would need to be about 0.25 rather than 0.036 at the same within-cell correlation, or the correlation would need to approach 1 at the same `v`) *and* its per-episode deviation would need to be persistent, which a fixed set of weights evaluated at drifting states is not. A seed ensemble on the full training data is the cheapest, and typically the narrowest, approximation to the parameter posterior, so this is a lower bound on epistemic width: a bootstrap-of-the-training-data ensemble (each member on a different resample of the 50 games) would test the data-uncertainty part, which five seeds on identical data do not touch (open follow-up, ~50 GPU-minutes). But at the measured width the ensemble is not a replacement for the copula, and the copula's rho is better read as a phenomenological residual (PR #140's "shared situation" the model does not track, plus the corner lock-in PR #170 documented) than as a stand-in for parameter uncertainty.

## 5. Notes

1. **Measured.** The five members are interchangeable with the shipped artifact: CV held-out log-loss 2.020–2.029 vs 2.0206, and the shipped trunk's distance from the ensemble mean (0.358 points, TV 0.158) equals a member's (0.378, 0.155). Nothing about seed 38381 is special, so the shipped copula calibration is a calibration on a typical member.
2. **Measured.** The disagreement is state-dependent, not global: median `sd_E` 0.27 points, q99 1.8, max 3.5. The wide tail sits where the marginal itself is wide (the correlation of `sd_E` with `sd_pred` is visible in the per-row parquet); on the frequent states the members agree to a quarter of a point.
3. **Measured.** The within-cell correlation of the seed deviations (0.16–0.24) is the copula's *shape* — members of one group are pushed the same way by a member's idiosyncrasies because they share the group state — but at a *size* that translates to rho 0.005–0.009. The two translations agree: the additive-shift arithmetic and the calibration script's own MLE on synthetic one-seed-per-episode data (0.0050 ± 0.0064 on train40, its own null floor 0.000–0.006). The synthetic estimate's spread across replicates (sd 0.006) is as large as its mean, so on a 40-episode sample the ensemble-as-noise dose is not even reliably distinguishable from zero by the calibration's estimator.
4. **Measured.** The copula rho refit on the human data with `P_bar` as marginal is 0.0404 [0.019, 0.059] against the shipped 0.0395 [0.018, 0.057]; per member it ranges 0.032–0.048. Averaging over seeds does not absorb the shared latent — a direct check that the seeds' shared error is not what the copula is fitting.
5. **Measured.** PR #140's residual within-group correlation "after removing state" was 0.073 from a *linear* fit; the same quantity given the GNN's teacher-forced expectation is 0.020 [0.005, 0.037] (ensemble mean) or 0.023 (shipped). The GNN tracks the shared situation better than the linear probe, and the residual shared part left for a sampler is correspondingly smaller — consistent with PR #179's finding that the group vnode absorbed a third of rho. Note this correlation is on the contribution scale and pair-weighted, not the copula's likelihood scale; it is the same object PR #140 reported, not a rho.
6. **Measured.** Persistence: lag-1 0.53, lag-2 0.34, lag-3 0.22, lag-5 0.15, lag-10 0.04; ICC over rounds 0.14; the copula script's cross-player lag-1 pairing gives a phi analogue of 0.73 (its own `phi_hat` on the humans was 0.81, CI [0.32, 1.84], ruled to 1.0). So a per-episode weight draw does *not* produce an episode-constant offset: the same weights meet a different state each round and disagree by a different amount and sign. The static-latent behaviour the copula needs for CG (PR #165, #179: `phi = 1` vs `0.618` vs none = CG 0.90 / 1.22 / 2.40) is not something an ensemble draw provides.
7. **Measured.** The bonus closed-loop run confirms the teacher-forced arithmetic without needing it: one member per episode scores CG 2.68 (copula 1.55, copula-free ablation 2.40), RCD 2.40 (1.31), the whole marginal C block leaves band <= 1 (CA/CC/CD/CF up 0.27–0.41) and the mean rises 18% to 1.224. Two rows improve — SB 1.006 -> 0.854 and SC 1.427 -> 1.003 — the same pair PR #179's no-copula ablation improved (SB 1.11 -> 0.90), i.e. the ensemble run is the copula-free run with a different seed of the marginal, not a new mechanism. RCE stays inside its band (0.894 -> 1.004) but the 15-19 withdrawal slope falls from -0.160 to -0.064; that is a protected-row halving under the §2 rule and would be a `[FAIL]` on its own if this were an experiment.
8. **Inferred.** Why the seeds disagree so little: the data are 100 doubled episodes of a 21-way categorical with a strongly informative `prev_contribution` feature, the trunk has hidden 20, and every member sees every game. Most of the posterior mass is pinned by the lag-1 stickiness; the parameter uncertainty that remains is about the rare states (high punishment at high contribution, the founding rounds), which is where `sd_E` reaches 2–3 points and where the RCB/RCE 15-19 band's instability across trainings (PR #183 note 5) comes from. A Bayesian treatment would sharpen *those* states' uncertainty, which matters for the response rows, not for the group-spread rows the copula targets.
9. **Inferred.** What would change the answer: (i) a data-bootstrap ensemble (bagging over games) measures data uncertainty on top of seed uncertainty and can only be wider; (ii) a posterior over the *group vnode's* state rather than over weights would be persistent by construction and is closer to what the copula's `z_cell` is — PR #159's per-group trained latent is that object, and it was dose-capped at 38% of the required move by its own likelihood, the same shortfall as here from the other direction. Both are follow-ups; neither is this branch's claim.
10. **Process.** The five jobs were submitted with `train_cluster.sh` into the isolated dir; `WANDB_MODE=disabled` was appended to that dir's `.env` while they were pending (PR #183's precedent; the canonical `.env` was not touched). The evaluation of the bonus sim had to run with `PYTHONPATH=<worktree>/src` — the shared local venv's editable install resolves `aimanager` to the main checkout, which lacks the RCE row and silently produces 21 rows. Remote dir `~/repros/ai-runs/copula-ensemble` stays until this PR closes.
