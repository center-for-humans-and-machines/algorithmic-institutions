The inflated emission: habits and extremes get their own dials

structured-head installed by PR #177 · contribution bay of the gaussian-MLP machine

The problem

People are creatures of habit and of extremes: they very often repeat exactly what they gave last round, or sit hard at 0 or at the maximum of 20. A smooth bell curve rounded into bins smears that probability around -- it cannot place a spike precisely on 'same as last time', so the round-to-round change row (RCA) stayed stuck.

The change

The output becomes a four-part mixture: the Gaussian body, plus three explicit probability atoms -- repeat last value, exactly 0, exactly 20 -- with weights the network learns for each situation.

The maths, in plain English

  1. The problem in one sentence: a bell curve, however well placed, spreads its probability over neighbouring values -- it cannot put a concentrated spike exactly on 'the same as last round', and humans produce that spike constantly.
  2. So the output becomes a mixture of four parts. Part 1 is the bell-curve body, made discrete honestly: level k gets the area of the curve between k - 0.5 and k + 0.5, and the areas beyond the ends are folded into levels 0 and 20.
  3. Parts 2-4 are 'atoms': mini-distributions that put ALL of their probability on one single level -- the member's previous contribution, exactly 0, and exactly 20.
  4. The same network that outputs mu and sigma also outputs four mixing weights, turned into positive numbers summing to 1 by a softmax (the standard trick for producing proportions). Final probability of level k = w_body x body(k) + w_repeat x [k = previous] + w_0 x [k = 0] + w_20 x [k = 20]. The weights depend on the situation, so the model itself decides, member by member and round by round, how much is habit and how much is drift.
  5. Because this is a genuine probability distribution over the 21 levels, training needs no tricks: minimise the ordinary 21-way cross-entropy against what members actually gave. The atoms only earn the probability mass the bell curve fails to explain -- if the body already predicts a repeat well, the repeat weight learns to stay small.
  6. Where it plugs in: it replaces the final output layer of the Gaussian-MLP trunk; everything upstream (features, hidden layer) and downstream (the group copula on the draws) is unchanged.
